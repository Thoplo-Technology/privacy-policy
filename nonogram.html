Clean Nonogram Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Clean Nonogram ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile nonogram (picross) puzzle game operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and you do not need to create an account to play.

Information the app uses

  • Game data on your device. Your puzzle progress, best times, and settings are stored locally on your device. They stay on the device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Usage analytics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate — for example which puzzles are opened and completed, and basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and general region. This helps us improve the game.
  • Diagnostics. We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and basic device information when the app encounters an error, used solely to find and fix bugs.

How we use information

  • To provide the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To understand overall usage so we can improve puzzles and features.
  • To keep the app stable by diagnosing crashes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising or for training AI models.

Sharing

  • Service providers. Analytics and crash reporting are provided by Google Firebase, which processes this data on our behalf. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings live on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase for a limited period according to Google's data-retention settings and are not tied to your identity.

Children

Clean Nonogram is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
