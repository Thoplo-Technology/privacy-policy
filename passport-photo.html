Passport & ID Photo Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Passport & ID Photo Maker ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile app operated by Thoplo Technology that helps you make passport, visa and ID photos and print-ready photo sheets. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: your photos are processed on your phone and are not uploaded to us. The app has no accounts. We only receive crash reports to fix bugs.

Information we collect

  • Photos you take or choose. The app uses your camera, or a photo you pick from your gallery, to make your passport or ID photo. Background removal, face detection and the photo check run entirely on your device. Your photos are not sent to our servers.
  • Saved photos. Photos you finish are kept in the app's private storage on your device ("My photos") so you can print them again later. They stay on your device.
  • Settings. Your language, country, paper size and similar preferences are stored on your device.
  • Country. To show your country's photo sizes first, the app reads your phone's region setting and, on Android, the country of your SIM card or mobile network. This is used on your device only and is not sent to us. The app does not use GPS or your precise location.
  • Diagnostics. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a crash report is sent via Google Firebase Crashlytics. It includes technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version and the error itself. Crash reports never include your photos.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and you do not need to create an account.

How we use information

  • To create, check and print your photos, entirely on your device.
  • To remember your saved photos and settings on your device.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs (crash reports only).

We do not sell your information, show you personalised ads, or use your photos to train AI models.

Sharing

  • When you choose to share. If you save a photo to your gallery, share a photo sheet (for example via WhatsApp, Viber or email), print it, or open maps to find a photo studio, the photo or request goes to the app or service you choose and is handled under that service's own privacy policy.
  • Service providers. Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports on our behalf. Google ML Kit, which performs on-device face detection and background removal, runs on your device; Google may receive limited usage and performance information about these features, but not your photos. App ratings are handled by Google Play or the App Store.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your photo in the app.
  • Photos / storage — to choose an existing photo and to save finished photos and sheets to your gallery.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings. You can still use the app with a photo from your gallery if camera access is off.

Data retention and deletion

Because your photos and settings stay on your device, you are in control of them:

  • Delete a single saved photo from My photos in the app.
  • Delete all saved photos from Settings → Clear all saved photos.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Photos you saved to your gallery or shared with other apps are not removed by the app; delete them there. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.

Security

Your photos never leave your device unless you choose to share them. Crash reports are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents may use the app to make photos of their children; those photos are processed on the device as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
