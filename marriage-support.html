Playing

How do I play?

Each player is dealt 21 cards from three decks. Build them into pure sequences, tunnelas (three identical cards) and dublees (pairs). Show three pure sequences or tunnelas, or seven dublees, to see the Maal: the Tiplu and the cards around it become your jokers and your Maal points. Declare when every card but one fits into a meld. To learn step by step, tap Learn to play for a short guided game.

Can I change the rules?

Yes. Marriage changes from family to family, so open House rules to choose Murder, Kidnap or Keep for unseen players' Maal, the dublee route, Man cards, the Alter, escalating or flat Maal points, the turn timer and how long a game lasts.

I closed the app in the middle of a game

Your game is saved as you play. Open the app again and tap Continue to carry on where you left off.

How do I switch between English and Nepali?

Open House rules and change Language · भाषा.

Wi-Fi games

How do I play with friends?

Everyone needs the app and the same Wi-Fi network. One player taps Wi-Fi game › Host a game and the others tap Join a game and type the host's PIN. No Wi-Fi? Turn on one phone's hotspot (on iPhone, Personal Hotspot) and have the others join it. No internet connection is needed.

My iPhone can't find the game

The first time you play on Wi-Fi, iPhone asks whether Marriage may find devices on your local network. If you said no, turn it on in the iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Marriage. Also check that every device is on the same Wi-Fi network; some guest and office networks keep devices apart, and a hotspot works instead.

"Wrong PIN"

The PIN has 5 digits and is shown on the host's screen. It comes from the host's place on the network, so it changes when the host moves to another Wi-Fi or hotspot: check the number the host sees now, and that you're on the same network.

Coins, themes and the point calculator

How do I get coins?

Coins are earned only by playing: finishing rounds, daily gifts, challenges and weekly quests. They unlock table themes and avatar frames. Coins can't be bought, sold or cashed out, and the app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

What is the point calculator?

It keeps the score for your games with real cards at home. Enter each player's Maal points and who saw and who won, and it works out the totals round after round. The app never handles any money.

Your data

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Your progress, profile, photo and statistics are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Do you collect personal information?

No account is needed and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which you can turn off in House rules › Crash reports and usage stats. See our Privacy Policy for details.
