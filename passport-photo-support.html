Passport Photo Maker Express Support

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Passport Photo Maker Express helps you make passport, visa and ID photos on your phone, by Thoplo Technology. On Google Play it is called Passport & ID Photo Maker. If you have a question, a problem or an idea, we'd like to hear from you.

Email: thoplotech@gmail.com

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, its system version, the app version (shown in Settings › About) and the document you are making a photo for.

Making your photo

My document isn't in the list. What can I do?

Choose Custom size and enter the width and height in millimeters or inches. Please email us the document and country so we can consider adding it.

The photo check shows a warning. What should I do?

Each line of the photo check tells you what to fix, such as moving closer, facing the light or keeping your head level. Fix what it asks and retake the photo if you need to.

Will my photo be accepted?

We can't promise that. Photo rules change and differ by country, and the authority that issues your document makes the final decision. Always confirm the current requirements with that authority before you apply. This app is not affiliated with any government.

Printing

My printed photos are the wrong size.

In the print settings, choose the same paper size as your sheet and set the scale to 100% or "Actual size". Do not use "Fit to page". Measure a printed photo with a ruler to check it. If a photo shop prints your sheet, ask them to print at actual size.

Can I put different sizes on one sheet?

Yes. You can combine sizes on one sheet, for example passport and stamp-size photos.

Which print margin should I choose?

Under Settings › Print margin, choose Photo lab to fit more photos, or Home printer for wider edges.

Online applications

How do I make a photo for an online form?

Open Online to make a JPEG. Some sizes have a preset. Otherwise enter the width, height and maximum size in KB that the website asks for, then tap Create JPEG.

My file is still over the limit.

Try a smaller pixel size, then tap Create again.

Your photos and data

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Your photos are processed on your phone and are not uploaded to us. They only leave your phone if you choose to share or print them.

How do I delete my photos?

Go to Settings › Clear all saved photos. This removes them from this app only, so photos you saved to your gallery stay there. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Does the app collect any data?

The app sends crash reports and technical diagnostics, never your photos. There is no account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings › Language. The app is available in English, Nepali and Hindi.
