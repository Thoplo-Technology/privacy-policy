Call Kitti Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Call Kitti ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile game of the Nepali card game Call Kitti, played against computer players or with friends on the same Wi-Fi, operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: the app collects nothing. You do not need an account, and there are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app never sends anything to us, or to anyone else, over the internet. Your name, settings and game stay on your device.

Information on your device

  • Your profile. The player name you type and the picture you choose from the app's built-in pictures. These are stored only on your device.
  • Game data. Your house rules, table theme, card back and other settings, the names and pictures you give the computer players, and an unfinished game, so that you can continue it later. These are stored only on your device.

Wi-Fi games

When you host or join a Wi-Fi game, your phone talks directly to the other players' phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot. Nothing goes over the internet or through our servers; we have none.

  • Your player name, your picture choice and the moves of the game (cards, sets and calls) are sent to the other phones in that game.
  • When you host a game, your player name is announced on that local network so that nearby phones can list your game.

Use a player name you are happy for the people playing with you to see.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Used only to connect to other phones on the same Wi-Fi for a Wi-Fi game. The app makes no connections to the internet.
  • Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast. Used to find your phone's address on the local network and to hear games announced by nearby phones.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID or any device identifier.

How we use information

We do not receive any information from the app. Everything it uses stays on your device, or goes only to the phones in a Wi-Fi game you choose to play.

Sharing

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or sign-in services, so no information is shared with any third party. We do not sell information, and we do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Data retention and deletion

Everything on your device stays there until you delete it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes all of it. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. If you have a question about your data, contact us at the address below.

No gambling

Call Kitti does not offer real-money gambling or betting. It has no coins and no in-app purchases. Points only keep score in the game.

Children

Call Kitti is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
