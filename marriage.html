Marriage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Marriage ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile game of the traditional Nepali card game Marriage, played against computer players or with friends on the same Wi-Fi, operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: you do not need an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. Your game, profile and photo stay on your device. To find and fix problems, the app sends crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to Google's Firebase, which you can turn off at any time in House rules → Crash reports and usage stats.

Information on your device

  • Your profile. The player name you type, the avatar you design (colours, flag and frame) and, if you choose to take one, a profile photo. These are stored only on your device. The photo is never uploaded or sent to anyone.
  • Game data. Your progress, level, statistics, achievements, coins, rewards, themes, house rules, settings and point-calculator records are stored only on your device.
  • Camera. The app uses the camera only when you tap Take a photo on the profile screen, to make your avatar photo. The picture is saved on your device and nowhere else.

Wi-Fi games

When you host or join a Wi-Fi game, your phone talks directly to the other players' phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot; nothing goes through our servers. Your player name, your drawn avatar (not your photo) and the moves of the game are sent to the other phones in that game. On iPhone, the app asks for permission to use the local network for this.

Crash reports and usage statistics

The app uses two Google services, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, to help us keep it working well:

  • Crash reports (Crashlytics). If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report with technical details: what went wrong in the app's code, the app version, your device model, operating system version and free memory, and a random identifier for this installation of the app.
  • Usage statistics (Analytics). The app sends events about how the game is played, such as a round being finished (whether it was won and the points), the lesson being started or finished, a level reached, a Wi-Fi game hosted or joined, the point calculator being used, a theme bought with in-game coins, and the language chosen. Google Analytics adds standard information such as the app version, device model, operating system, approximate region (worked out from your internet connection, not from GPS) and a random identifier for this installation.

These reports never contain your player name, profile photo or the cards in your hand. The app does not use your device's advertising ID, and it has no advertising.

You can turn crash reports and usage statistics off, and on again, with House rules → Crash reports and usage stats. When it is off, the app sends nothing to Firebase.

How we use information

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings on your device.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand, in total, how the game is played (for example, how many players finish the lesson), so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Sharing

Crash reports and usage statistics are processed by Google on our behalf as part of Firebase, under Google's privacy policy. We do not share any other information with third parties. In Wi-Fi games, the information described above goes only to the phones in that game.

Data retention and deletion

Everything on your device stays there until you delete it, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days. Google Analytics keeps detailed usage data for at most 14 months; after that only totals remain, which say nothing about any one player. Because this data is linked only to a random installation identifier, not to you, we cannot tell which data is yours. You can stop it being collected at any time in the house rules, and reinstalling the app gives it a new identifier. If you have a question about your data, contact us at the address below.

No gambling

Marriage does not offer real-money gambling or betting, and it has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and can be spent only on looks in the game, such as table themes; they cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. The point calculator can show totals at a rate you choose for your own games played with real cards, but the app does not handle, collect or pay out any money.

Children

Marriage is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
