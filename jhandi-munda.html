Jhandi Munda 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Jhandi Munda 3D ("the app", "we", "us") is a 3D dice roller for the traditional Nepali game Jhandi Munda (Langur Burja), operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: the app works entirely offline and does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and you do not need to create an account.

Information the app uses

  • Roll history on your device. The results of your dice rolls, with the time of each roll, are saved in a local database on your device so you can look back at them in the History tab. This data never leaves your device.
  • Settings on your device. Your sound on/off preference is stored locally on your device.

The app does not request internet access, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, files or contacts.

How we use information

  • To show your past rolls in the History tab.
  • To remember your sound preference between sessions.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising or for training AI models.

Sharing

Because no data leaves your device, we do not share any information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your roll history and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can erase your roll history at any time with Clear History in the History tab. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

No gambling

Jhandi Munda 3D is a dice roller only. It does not offer real-money gambling, betting, in-app purchases or prizes.

Children

Jhandi Munda 3D does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
