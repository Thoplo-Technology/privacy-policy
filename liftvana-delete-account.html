Delete Your Liftvana Account

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Liftvana is a workout and nutrition app by Thoplo Technology (liftvana.com). You can delete your Liftvana account and all of its data at any time, either in the app or by contacting us.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Liftvana app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete account again.

Your account and data are deleted from our servers immediately, the app's data is cleared from your device, and you are signed out.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email contact@liftvana.com with the subject "Delete my Liftvana account". Send it from the email address you use for Liftvana (including the Google account you sign in with), so we can confirm the account is yours. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, and sign-in details (including the link to your Google account, if you used Google Sign-In).
  • All your fitness data: workout plans, routines, exercises, workouts, sets, reps and weights.
  • Your nutrition and food logs.
  • Your preferences, such as your weight unit.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What may be kept

  • Standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) created when your device connected to our servers. These are used only to keep the service secure and are deleted on a rolling basis.
  • Information we are required to keep by law, if any.

Data stored on your own device is removed when you delete your account in the app, sign out, or uninstall the app.

More information

See the Liftvana Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and why. Questions: contact@liftvana.com
