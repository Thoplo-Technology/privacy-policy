All Fonts apps for Windows — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the font apps published in the Microsoft Store by Sabthok Digital under names such as All Hindi Fonts, All Nepali Fonts, All Arabic Fonts and the other "All … Fonts" apps for each language ("the apps", "we", "us"). Each app lets you browse, preview and install fonts for one language from its companion website. This policy explains what information the apps use, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: the apps have no accounts and do not ask for your name, email or any personal details. Your favourites, preview text and downloaded fonts stay on your PC. The apps only contact their own font website to fetch the font list and font files.

Information the apps use

  • Font catalogue and font files. To show fonts, each app downloads the font list, font details, preview images and the font files you choose from its companion website (for example hindi-fonts.com for All Hindi Fonts). As with any website visit, that server receives your IP address and basic technical information such as the app name and version. It is used to deliver the fonts and keep the service secure, and is kept in standard server logs.
  • Favourites and preview text. Fonts you mark as favourites and the sample text you type to preview fonts are stored on your PC only. They are not sent to us.
  • Downloaded fonts. Fonts you download are saved in your Downloads folder (in a folder named after the app) and a copy is cached in the app's own storage so previews load quickly. To install a font, the app opens it in Windows Font Viewer; installation is done by Windows.
  • Microsoft Store. Downloads, updates, ratings, reviews and any purchases or subscriptions are handled by Microsoft. Microsoft provides us with aggregated reports, such as install counts, usage and crash statistics, which do not identify you. Microsoft's handling of this data is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, files or location, and the apps do not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use information

  • To show the font catalogue and deliver the fonts you choose.
  • To remember your favourites and preview text on your PC.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the apps are used and find crashes (through Microsoft's reports).

We do not sell your information or use it for personalised advertising.

Purchases

If an app offers a paid upgrade or subscription, payment is processed by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We do not receive your payment card details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Microsoft account.

Links to websites

The apps include links to their companion font website, for example a font's page or the website's own terms. When you open these links, your browser visits that website and its own privacy policy applies.

Fonts and licences

Fonts are made by their designers and are distributed under their own licences, shown on each font's page. Please check a font's licence before using it commercially.

Data retention and deletion

  • Remove a favourite in the app at any time.
  • Delete downloaded fonts from your Downloads folder, and uninstall fonts in Windows Settings → Personalization → Fonts.
  • Uninstalling an app removes its stored favourites, preview text and cached files from your PC.

Server logs on the font websites are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting and then deleted.

Security

The apps communicate with their font websites over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
