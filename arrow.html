Clean Arrow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Clean Arrow ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile arrow puzzle game operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Clean Arrow does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, you do not need to create an account to play, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information the app uses

  • Game data on your device. Your level progress, stars, hints, daily puzzle streak and settings are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Daily reminder. If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a reminder on your device. The reminder is created entirely on the device; no data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn it off in the app or in your device's Settings at any time.
  • Ratings. The app may occasionally show Apple's standard prompt asking you to rate it. This prompt is handled by Apple, and we do not receive any information from it beyond the public rating or review you choose to leave on the App Store.
  • Game Center. If Game Center features are enabled and you are signed in to Game Center, the app may submit scores such as levels completed and your daily streak to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we do not receive your Game Center account details.

How we use information

Information stays on your device and is used only to run the game, save your progress, and remind you about the daily puzzle if you ask it to. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties, because the app does not send any information to us. Features provided by Apple (notifications, ratings and Game Center) are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

All game data lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We hold no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Clean Arrow is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
