Clean Arrow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Clean Arrow ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile arrow puzzle game operated by Thoplo Technology. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Clean Arrow does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, you do not need to create an account to play, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information the app uses

  • Game data on your device. Your level progress, stars, hints, picture album, daily puzzle streak and settings are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • iCloud sync. If iCloud is turned on for the app, your game progress (not your settings or unfinished boards) is also kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it follows you to your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. This copy is stored by Apple, is private to your account, and we cannot see or access it.
  • Daily reminder. If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a reminder on your device. The reminder is created entirely on the device; no data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn it off in the app or in your device's Settings at any time.
  • Ratings. The app may occasionally show Apple's standard prompt asking you to rate it. This prompt is handled by Apple, and we do not receive any information from it beyond the public rating or review you choose to leave on the App Store.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your scores (levels completed and your best daily streak) to Game Center leaderboards and reports the achievements you earn. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we do not receive your Game Center account details. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings at any time.

How we use information

Information stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you use iCloud, and is used only to run the game, save and sync your progress, and remind you about the daily puzzle if you ask it to. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties, because the app does not send any information to us. Features provided by Apple (iCloud, notifications, ratings and Game Center) are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you use iCloud, the synced copy of your progress stays in your iCloud account so it can be restored if you reinstall. To delete your progress everywhere, use Settings → Reset Progress in the app, which clears it on all your devices and in iCloud. We hold no copy of any of it, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Clean Arrow is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
